Research

My work follows three questions. For each I give the idea in a sentence or two and the papers that carry it. The complete record is under Publications.

Neural representations and computation

What the cells of a brain, or the units of a network, represent, and what that representation makes easy to compute.

Place Cells as Multi-Scale Position Embeddings. NeurIPS 2025

The population of place cells forms an embedding of position whose inner products are normalized random-walk transition probabilities. In this embedding, planning follows a straight path.

(1)

Latent Thought Models with Variational Bayes Inference-Time Computation. ICML 2025

A language model with explicit thought vectors, inferred for each sequence by fast learning of local parameters, which complements the slow learning of global ones.

Learning, reasoning, and planning

How a model learns from experience, and how it uses what it has learned to think ahead.

Learning to Plan from Random Exploration. Preprint, 2026

Planning can be based on random exploration directly, without policy optimization.

Latent Plan Transformer for Trajectory Abstraction. NeurIPS 2024

A latent plan controls a whole trajectory. Only the whole plan is evaluated, without stepwise rewards, which avoids the credit assignment problem.
