Research

Two models carry most of my work. For each I give the model, the idea of each paper in a sentence or two, and a figure. The complete record is under Publications.

Thinking, planning, and design as inference

A latent vector stands behind a whole object , such as a passage of text, a trajectory, or a molecule, and behind its value . To think, to plan, or to design is then to infer .

(1)

Latent Thought Models with Variational Bayes Inference-Time Computation. ICML 2025

A language model with explicit thought vectors, inferred for each sequence by fast learning of local parameters, which complements the slow learning of global ones.

Latent Plan Transformer for Trajectory Abstraction. NeurIPS 2024

A latent plan controls a whole trajectory. Only the whole plan is evaluated, without stepwise rewards, which avoids the credit assignment problem.

Molecule Design by Latent Prompt Transformer. NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight

A continuous latent design is easier to optimize than a search over the discrete space of molecules.

A learned prior. The prior is itself learned. In the energy-based form, a function tilts a simple reference distribution toward the latent vectors the data support.

(2)

Molecule Design by Latent Space Energy-based Modeling and Gradual Distribution Shifting. UAI 2023

Design starts from existing molecules and shifts the model gradually toward the desired property.

Latent Space Energy-based Neural ODEs. TMLR 2025

An energy-based prior over the initial state of a neural ODE in the latent space, for continuous-time sequences.

Representation as geometry

What the cells of a brain, or the units of a network, represent, and what that representation makes easy to compute.

Place Cells as Multi-Scale Position Embeddings. NeurIPS 2025

The population of place cells forms an embedding of position whose inner products are normalized random-walk transition probabilities. In this embedding, planning follows a straight path.

(3)

Learning to Plan from Random Exploration. Preprint, 2026

Planning can be based on random exploration directly, without policy optimization. Learning the transition kernel gives emergent cognitive maps.

A Minimalistic Representation Model for Head Direction System. CogSci 2025

A minimal model in which rotation of the head acts on the representation as a group. Gaussian-like tuning and accurate path integration emerge.

A Statistical Approach to Topological Entanglement. Preprint, 2023

Topological entanglement entropy read as a high-order mutual information, which a restricted Boltzmann machine can learn from measured samples.
