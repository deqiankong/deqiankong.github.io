Fonts on this site
==================

The fonts in this folder are MODIFIED versions of Latin Modern, version 2.004,
by B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki (GUST e-foundry).

  Copyright 2003, 2009 B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX users
  groups). This work is released under the GUST Font License -- see
  http://tug.org/fonts/licenses/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt for details.

They are not Latin Modern, they are distributed under different names, and they
are not supported by the original authors. Please do not report problems with
them to GUST.

  KongModern-regular.woff2       from lmroman10-regular.otf
  KongModern-italic.woff2        from lmroman10-italic.otf
  KongModern-bold.woff2          from lmroman10-bold.otf
  KongModernMono-regular.woff2   from lmmono10-regular.otf

    Changes: every outline was moved outward by 5.5/1000 em, so each stroke is
    thicker by 11/1000 em. Advance widths, kerning and other layout tables are
    unchanged; hinting instructions were dropped and the recorded stem widths
    and alignment zones adjusted. The fonts were renamed "Kong Modern" and
    "Kong Modern Mono", subset to Latin letters and common punctuation, and
    converted to WOFF2.
    The script that makes them is tools/make_font.py in this site's repository.

Unmodified originals: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/latin-modern


Equations
=========

The equations on the pages are drawings, not fonts: outlines of glyphs from the
BaKoMa Computer Modern fonts (as shipped with matplotlib), thickened by the same
11/1000 em and embedded in the pages as SVG.
